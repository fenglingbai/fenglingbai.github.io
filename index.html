About Me

I am a researcher at Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, focusing on biomedical image analysis with an emphasis on electron microscopy (EM) image analysis, neuron segmentation, synapse reconstruction, and connectomics.

My research aims to develop advanced computational methods for understanding brain connectivity at the nanoscale, leveraging deep learning and computer vision techniques to process large-scale volumetric EM data.
